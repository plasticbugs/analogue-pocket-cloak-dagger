Cloak & Dagger (Atari, 1983)

Agent X raids Dr. Boom's underground
bomb factory. Walk with one hand,
fire with the other.

* two 6502s: one runs the game, one
  does nothing but draw into a
  256x256 bitmap through an auto-
  incrementing address generator
* two POKEYs into op-amp summing
  stages, modelled from the ladder
  up rather than as a linear mixer
* 256x232 at 61.04 Hz, the board's
  own rate, off its timing PROM
* settings and high scores are kept
  in the machine's real 512-byte
  NVRAM and saved to cloak.sav

Controls
  D-pad     walk
  X A B Y   fire up right down left
            (two at once = diagonal)
  L or R    igniter
  Select    coin
  Start     start

ROMs are not included. Build the
image from your own MAME romset:

  python3 mra_build.py cloak.mra
      cloak.zip

and copy cloak.rom into
Assets/cloak/common/.
